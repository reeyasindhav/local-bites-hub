project Description:
A platform for food lovers to discover and review local street food stalls, food trucks, and hidden gem eateries.

Design Idea: Vibrant street-culture aesthetic, map-based stall finder, dish photo cards, vendor profile pages, neighbourhood food trail feature.

Problem Statement:

Mainstream food discovery apps primarily focus on formal, dine-in restaurants, leaving informal street food stalls, food trucks, and hidden neighbourhood gems largely unmapped, unreviewed, and difficult to locate. Because these small-scale vendors lack a dedicated digital presence, food enthusiasts struggle to discover authentic local street food, verify real-time operating locations, or find trustworthy community recommendations. Localbite fills this gap with a dedicated, community-driven platform that includes map-based tracking, dish-level visuals, curated neighbourhood food trails, and vendor profiles to make authentic hyperlocal street food easy to find.
